# Pawabase Core design system

The approved identity is the circular Core mark and outlined **Pawabase** wordmark from `pawabase-logo-kit`. Use the supplied vectors; do not redraw, rotate, stretch, recolor, add effects, or fill the crescent opening.

## Palette

| Ink | Value | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | `#101315` | Dark canvas, text and controls on light backgrounds |
| Graphite | `#34383F` | Borders, secondary logo shape on dark backgrounds |
| Gray | `#70757D` | Secondary logo shape on light backgrounds, metadata |
| Warm white | `#F5F3EE` | Primary text and actions on dark backgrounds, light canvas |

Use only these four inks. Surface, hover, focus, and subdued text tones may be made with opacity or `color-mix` between these inks. No orange, lavender, colored status fills, or multicolor chart palettes. Show status with explicit labels, icons and line styles as well as tonal contrast. Keep primary text and controls clearly legible.

## Logo and icons

Use `horizontal-dark.svg` on dark surfaces and `horizontal-light.svg` on light surfaces. Symbol size is at least 24px, horizontal width at least 156px, with clear space of one quarter of the symbol diameter. Use the dedicated adaptive favicon and supplied touch icon. Provider logos remain third-party identities.

## Components

Primary actions use warm white with ink text on dark surfaces, and ink with warm-white text on light surfaces. Secondary actions use a subtle tonal fill and graphite border. Keyboard focus has a visible warm-white or ink outline with an offset. Disabled actions use reduced opacity.

Use fine borders and small tonal steps to distinguish canvas, panels and raised surfaces. Preserve existing responsive grids and interaction patterns. Keep a 4px spacing rhythm; table sections have 16px separation. Use the existing product typeface, strong display headings, and clear secondary copy. Code uses weight and neutral contrast rather than syntax hues.

Keep the product interface dark. Both marketing websites and the product use the dark palette throughout. The inverse logo assets are available for light documents.
