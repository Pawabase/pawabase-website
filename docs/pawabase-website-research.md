# Pawabase website research

Research date: 2026-10-06. This is a living audit; observations are limited to the references successfully accessed in this pass. The remaining requested references have not yet been visually audited and are intentionally not represented as researched.

## Railway

- Leads with an outcome, then uses product evidence and progressively more specific capability stories.
- Its long page works because each chapter has a distinct task: deployment, networking, observability, and collaboration.
- Useful for Pawabase: make each section answer a business question before introducing the corresponding backend capability.
- Do not borrow: Railway’s cloud/deployment language, customer claims, usage metrics, or visual system.

## Resend

- Uses a sharply scoped promise and immediately gives a concrete interface/code artifact that proves the promise.
- Product screens are used as evidence, not as decorative wallpaper. Copy stays close to the practical activity being shown.
- Useful for Pawabase: pair Studio imagery only with the relevant part of the story; explain a specific application responsibility rather than listing services.
- Do not borrow: Resend’s email-specific code treatments, red/black branding, or their proof points.

## Linear

- Presents a highly controlled product world: editorial pacing, strong typography, and interfaces placed at moments where they explain a workflow.
- Useful for Pawabase: use fewer but larger visual statements; let the page alternate between quiet copy and focused product/environment imagery.
- Do not borrow: Linear’s motion language, gradients, layout, or task-management metaphors.

## Attio

- Couples product framing with a business outcome and gives each visual a clear role in the narrative.
- Useful for Pawabase: explain how a backend capability supports a real product scenario—orders, accounts, documents, notifications—not merely the capability name.
- Do not borrow: Attio’s CRM-specific narratives, visual grammar, or interface treatment.

## Pawabase direction derived from the audit

1. Lead with the cost of rebuilding recurring backend responsibilities, then describe the foundation Pawabase provides.
2. Organize capability stories around application work: accounts and permissions; records and files; actions that continue after a request; external systems.
3. Use the Cloud Kingdom and Little Automaton only as distinctive supporting brand devices. They must not replace product explanation.
4. Use authentic Studio material sparingly and never fabricate product activity, statistics, or integrations.
5. Favor large editorial compositions, readable hierarchy, purposeful transitions, and responsive re-composition over repeated cards or decorative technical diagrams.
