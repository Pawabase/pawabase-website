# Pawabase visual rebuild direction

## Audit of the current page

The current implementation relies on a hero with a Studio panel, repeated dark editorial blocks, two-up grids, and decorative mascot placements. It lacks enough sectional contrast, uses product imagery as a surface rather than evidence, and does not create a coherent progression from business problem to product capability.

## Art direction

Pawabase becomes an architectural product story: quiet ink surfaces, precise lavender accents, real Studio captures used as evidence, and the Cloud Kingdom used as a recurring environmental transition rather than a backdrop on every section. The Little Automaton appears three times only: introduction, a business-process scene, and closing.

## Section architecture

1. **Hero — product before infrastructure:** split editorial composition; message about not rebuilding the same backend work.
2. **The visible product / invisible work:** customer-facing product silhouette above its supporting responsibilities; scroll-revealed layers.
3. **Foundation:** asymmetric, non-card capability composition organised by what an application needs to do.
4. **Accounts and access:** typography-led policy story with a restrained mascot intervention.
5. **Records and files:** full-bleed application-data landscape paired with a real Studio crop.
6. **Business logic:** an interactive, semantically correct order lifecycle from request to background follow-up.
7. **Work after the request:** dense editorial time-line for queues, schedules, mail, and workers.
8. **External systems:** webhook-to-application process story, without unverified integration claims.
9. **Products built on Pawabase:** diagonal scenario gallery for SaaS, marketplace, commerce, internal tools, and mobile.
10. **Studio:** authentic screenshot as a single proof point, with an explanatory annotation layer.
11. **Infrastructure choice:** quiet environmental deployment-control composition.
12. **Growth of the application:** evolving-requirements editorial sequence.
13. **Closing:** immersive Cloud Kingdom horizon with concise conversion choices.

## Motion and responsiveness

Only use scroll reveals on scene layers, one sticky business-process scene, image parallax under pointer/scroll, and button/interactive-state feedback. Mobile recomposes every split layout into deliberate narrative order; it does not shrink desktop compositions.
